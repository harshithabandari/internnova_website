How long does an internship last?

InternNova's internship programs are six weeks long, with a structured path through learning and hands-on project work.

Are the programs virtual?

Yes. The programs are designed to be completed virtually, so you can learn and build from wherever you are.

What will I work on?

Each track is built around practical learning and industry-oriented tasks. Your projects will depend on the domain you choose.

Will I receive a certificate?

Internship tracks include a verified certificate on completion. Check the specific program details for what's included.

Can I get help choosing a track?

Start with the subject that makes you curious. If you're still unsure, reach out to the InternNova team for guidance.
